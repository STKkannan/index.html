01 / About

Decoding disease resistance
in wheat & barley.

My doctoral research focuses on “An Integrated Disease Resistance Omics Approach in Wheat and Barley Against Spot Blotch and Bacterial Leaf Streak.”

I am a PhD student and Graduate Research Assistant at South Dakota State University, working in the Plant Pathogen Interaction Lab (Dr. Gazala Ameen). My research integrates genomics, transcriptomics, molecular genetics and bioinformatics to understand crop disease resistance and support resilient crop improvement.

My earlier research spans wheat stress genomics and association mapping, sorghum shoot fly resistance and the characterization of barnyard millet mutants.
