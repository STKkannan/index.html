01 / About

Decoding wheat and barley disease resistance mechanisms.

My doctoral research focuses on “An Integrated Disease Resistance Omics Approach in Wheat and Barley Against Spot Blotch and Bacterial Leaf Streak” at South Dakota State University.

Graduate Research Assistant, Plant Pathogen Interaction Lab (Dr. Gazala Ameen). My work integrates disease phenotyping, genomics, transcriptomics and bioinformatics to investigate crop disease resistance.

02 / Research directions

Research focus: genetics, genomics and crop disease resistance.

WHEAT & BARLEY

Disease Resistance Genetics

Genetic dissection of spot blotch, bacterial leaf streak and rust resistance through phenotyping, association mapping and candidate-region analysis.

GENOME-WIDE MAPPING

GWAS, QTLs & Haplotypes

Mapping resistance-associated loci, evaluating haplotype diversity and prioritizing genomic regions for crop improvement.

TRANSCRIPTOMICS

Host–Pathogen Interactions

Comparative RNA-seq and functional interpretation of host responses, susceptibility mechanisms and defense-associated pathways.

FUNCTIONAL GENOMICS

Candidate Genes & Orthologs

Integrating gene annotation, comparative genomics and ortholog analysis to prioritize plausible resistance-associated genes.

SORGHUM

Insect Resistance & Metabolomics

Investigating shoot fly resistance through molecular breeding, genetic markers and plant biochemical traits.

BIOINFORMATICS

Reproducible Genomics

Analysis of genotype, phenotype and sequencing datasets using statistical genomics and reproducible computational workflows.
