01 / About

Research at the intersection of genetics and plant immunity.

I am a PhD researcher in Plant Science at South Dakota State University, studying disease resistance in wheat and barley through integrated genomics and molecular plant–pathogen interactions. My background spans genome-wide association studies, haplotype analysis, transcriptomics, molecular breeding, and metabolomics.

My doctoral research focuses on an integrated disease-resistance omics approach in wheat and barley against spot blotch and bacterial leaf streak. My earlier research at Tamil Nadu Agricultural University investigated sorghum shoot fly resistance.

02 / Research

Research focus

Wheat Disease Resistance

GWAS, multi-trait association mapping, haplotype analysis, and marker validation for resistance traits.

Expression profiling, candidate gene prioritization, and disease-associated molecular networks.

Identifying genetic loci and favorable allelic combinations for crop improvement.

Marker-assisted breeding and metabolomics approaches for shoot fly resistance.

Computational analysis of genomic and transcriptomic datasets and reproducible research workflows.
