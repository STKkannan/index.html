01 / About

Decoding disease resistance
in wheat & barley.

My doctoral research focuses on “An Integrated Disease Resistance Omics Approach in Wheat and Barley Against Spot Blotch and Bacterial Leaf Streak.”

I am a PhD student and Graduate Research Assistant at South Dakota State University, working in the Plant Pathogen Interaction Lab (Dr. Gazala Ameen). My research integrates genomics, transcriptomics, molecular genetics and bioinformatics to understand crop disease resistance and support resilient crop improvement.

My earlier research spans wheat stress genomics and association mapping, sorghum shoot fly resistance and the characterization of barnyard millet mutants.

02 / Research directions

Questions that drive my research.

From resistance loci to molecular networks, translating biological complexity into actionable insights for crop improvement.

01 / Research

Disease Resistance Genomics

GWAS, haplotype analysis and genetic dissection of resistance to bacterial leaf streak, rusts and other crop diseases.

02 / Research

Host–Pathogen Interactions

Understanding molecular recognition, susceptibility mechanisms and defense responses in wheat and barley.

03 / Research

Comparative Transcriptomics

Expression profiling and defense-associated gene networks in the barley–spot blotch pathosystem.

04 / Research

Candidate Genes & Functional Genomics

Prioritization of resistance loci, wall-associated kinases and putative functional variants.

05 / Research

Molecular Breeding & Marker Development

Marker validation, KASP genotyping and genomic approaches supporting crop improvement.

06 / Research

Integrative Multiomics

Connecting genetic variation, transcriptomics, molecular networks and phenotype data to interpret disease resistance.
