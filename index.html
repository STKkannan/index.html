01 / About

Decoding the genetics of crop resilience.

I am a PhD researcher in Plant Science at South Dakota State University, investigating disease resistance in wheat and barley using integrative genomics and molecular plant–pathogen interaction approaches.

My doctoral research focuses on spot blotch and bacterial leaf streak. My previous research includes wheat stress genomics at ICAR–National Bureau of Plant Genetic Resources and sorghum shoot fly resistance research at Tamil Nadu Agricultural University.

Doctoral advisor: Dr. Gazala Ameen
Expected graduation: December 2028
